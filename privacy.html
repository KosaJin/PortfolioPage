Privacy

This site has no cookies of its own, no analytics, no forms, no accounts and no ads. I don't collect any information about visitors.

It does load a few things from other companies. When it does, they can see your IP address and browser details:

  • GitHub hosts the site and sees every request, as any web host does.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces.
  • YouTube supplies the videos, through its youtube-nocookie.com address. Its player may collect data once you press play.

Links to ArtStation and LinkedIn lead to sites with their own policies.

If you email me, I see your address and message, and use them only to reply.
